Detailed Description

template<typename... CallableArgs>
class BT::ThreadSafeSignal< CallableArgs >

A signal supporting concurrent notification and subscription. Callbacks run outside the subscription lock and may be invoked concurrently. Dropping a subscriber prevents future calls, but does not wait for callbacks already selected for invocation. The owner must synchronize their lifetime with callback targets.
